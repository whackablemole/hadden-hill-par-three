# Hadden Hill Par Three Scorekeeper

## Overview

A simple web-based app with Google login for tracking scores and stats on the Hadden Hill Par Three course.

## Par 3 Course Overview

Hadden Hill's Par Three course is 6 holes long but can be repeated to achieve up to 18 holes worth of golf.

## Holes

| Hole # | Length (yards) | Stroke Index |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| 1      | 150            | 3            |
| 2      | 85             | 5            |
| 3      | 140            | 1            |
| 4      | 95             | 2            |
| 5      | 115            | 4            |
| 6      | 105            | 6            |

## Tracking for rounds

- **Date**
- Total holes (6, 12 or 18)
- Total strokes
- Average putts per hole
- Total putts
- Total birdies
- Total pars
- Total bogeys
- Total double bogeys
- Total triple bogey+

## Tracking for each hole

- Strokes
- Penalties
- Bunkers
- Putts
- Green in regulation

## Functional Requirements

- Google oauth login available
- User can use the app whilst they are going round the course to save their progress live
- User can see their stats after the round
- User can see their overall stats showing all their rounds and totals
- User can remove rounds from their history
